# Electronics — photo sources

Every image in this folder came from **Unsplash**, downloaded under the free
Unsplash licence. Marwan's standing rule is Unsplash or Pexels only — never
Pinterest, never Google Images — and the source URL for each file is recorded
below so anyone can go back to the original.

The listings these are attached to belong to **Bright Spark Electronics**, a
placeholder store created for the pre-launch preview. The prices are invented
and every product row carries `price_is_placeholder = true`.

## Two products deliberately have NO photo

**Instant Print Camera** and **Digital Photo Frame** were left without an
image on purpose. A missing photo reads as "not shot yet", which is true. A
wrong one is a promise about what arrives in the box.

- **Instant camera.** Every free instant-camera photo on Unsplash is of a
  Fujifilm Instax or a Polaroid, with the wordmark plainly readable on the
  front of the camera in each one. Six were downloaded and opened; all six
  showed the brand. Putting a branded camera on a listing from a shop that
  does not carry that brand is exactly what migration 0054 banned.
- **Digital photo frame.** Unsplash has no photograph of one. Searches for
  "digital photo frame" and "digital frame screen" return ordinary wooden
  picture frames, tablets and laptops. Nothing in the results is the product.

## Every one of these was opened and looked at

Each file was downloaded and viewed before it was attached, and several
finalists were re-fetched zoomed in on the product to check for a logo the
full-size view hid. That step is not optional here: a previous session
attached ten Sport photos from a hard-coded list of Unsplash ids without
opening any of them, and put an empty American football field on "Goalkeeper
Gloves" and a lifestyle sneaker on "Running Trainers".

## Photos rejected, and why

Brand marks matter more in electronics than anywhere else, because most
product photography of headphones and cameras is of one specific branded
model. These were all downloaded, opened, and thrown away:

- Two over-ear pairs with **SONY** across the headband, and one with **B&O**.
- A pair of **AirPods Max** — no logo in frame, but the silhouette is the
  brand.
- A **JBL** portable speaker, and a **SONY** speaker with the name on its
  wrist strap.
- A flat-lay of headphones on yellow that looked clean at full size; zoomed
  in, **JBL** is embossed on the ear cup. Rejected on the zoom.
- Earbuds carrying the **boAt** triangle on each bud.
- Two **Apple Watches** and a **Xiaomi Mi Band** retail box.
- A silver power bank with **gomi power bank · 10,000 mAh** printed legibly
  along the top.
- A "tech flat lay" considered for the hero: **Audio-Technica ATH-M50x** on
  the headband and an **Apple** logo on the phone case.

Also avoided: **Unsplash+ premium** images (`plus.unsplash.com/premium_photo-…`).
Three shortlisted photos turned out to be premium rather than free-licence.
They fail to download server-side, which is a useful accident — but they
should be excluded on licence grounds regardless.

## One thing worth Marwan's eye

`05_smart_watch.jpg` is shot on black gravel with flecks of broken glass —
a rugged, outdoorsy mood rather than a gifting one. It was chosen because it
is the only round smart watch on Unsplash with no brand anywhere on it. If he
would rather the tone were softer, that is the photo to swap first.

## The two blanks, filled 2026-08-15

Marwan's instruction: no item left without a photo. Both were resolved, but
only one of them by finding a picture.

| File | Listed as | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `instant_print_camera.jpg` | Instant Print Camera | https://www.pexels.com/photo/17378226/ | Pexels, free commercial |
| `portable_mini_projector.jpg` | Portable Mini Projector | https://www.pexels.com/photo/31726737/ | Pexels, free commercial |

**Instant Print Camera — found.** Unsplash only carries visibly branded Instax
and Polaroid bodies, which is why it was left blank. Pexels had a white
instant camera whose front plate and lens ring are genuinely unmarked. Correct
item, no legible brand, attached unchanged.

**Digital Photo Frame — the PRODUCT changed, not the photo.** Neither Unsplash
nor Pexels has a photograph of a digital photo frame; both return wall picture
frames, laptops and tablets. Two independent sources, same answer, so the
listing could not be photographed honestly at all.

Rather than hang a wooden picture frame on an electronics listing — the exact
mistake that put an empty American football field on "Goalkeeper Gloves" — the
product became a **Portable Mini Projector**: a real, giftable item, still
placeholder stock, and the photo genuinely shows the thing the listing
describes. The price ($80) carried over and is still invented.

Both files were downloaded, opened and looked at before being attached.
