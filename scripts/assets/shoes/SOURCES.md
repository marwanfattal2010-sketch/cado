# Shoes — photo sources

Shoes was the one category the section work could not rescue: with two
products there is nothing for a deals strip or a Top-of rail to honestly
show, and no layout change fixes that. It needed stock, and stock needed
photographs that are not Nike.

## What worked, after a lot of what didn't

STUDIO SHOTS ON WHITE. Searching "sneakers" returns Nike and Adidas almost
exclusively — the same wall that made Sport take three attempts. Searching
for the FORM instead (ankle boot, platform heel, strappy sandal) and adding
"product" or "white background" returns catalogue photography, which is
usually of unbranded samples.

## Rejected, each one opened at full size

- A Nike Air Force 1 whose alt text read "a pair of white shoes". Alt text
  hides brands; only looking finds them. This is the second time that exact
  shoe has been caught this way.
- A Getty/iStock-branded duffel bag, logos all over the strap.
- Legs hanging out of a car window in heels — editorial, not a product.
- Worn brown work boots, visibly scuffed and dirty. Unbranded and well shot,
  but a shop selling new goods should not illustrate them with used ones.

## One judgement call

`strappy-sandals.jpg` has "LAZZARI" legible on the insole. Kept, on the same
reasoning as the skipping rope's "ata" stamp: the rule is not "no lettering
anywhere", it is "do not put a MAJOR brand on stock the shop does not carry".
A swoosh implies Cedar Street sells Nike. An obscure Italian label on an
insole implies nothing to a shopper in Beirut. If Marwan disagrees, this is
the one to pull.
