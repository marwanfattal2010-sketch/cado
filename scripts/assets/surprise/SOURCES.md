## Every one of these was looked at, not assumed

Each file was downloaded and opened before it was attached to a listing. That
step is not optional here: the previous batch of seed photos was attached on
the strength of filenames alone, and it put an empty American football field
on "Goalkeeper Gloves" and a lifestyle sneaker on "Running Trainers".

## Photos deliberately NOT used

- **The reel cover with the plush bears** (the one an earlier note called
  "grid index 0 — ready gift boxes with printed mugs"). It is not that. It is
  a video cover: a person holding plush toys with the word SURPRISE burned
  across the top. Grid positions from that note no longer point anywhere —
  the shop has posted since.
- **The 40th Birthday Survival Kit hamper** (https://www.instagram.com/p/DW1gYiSjcjr/).
  A genuinely good photo, but the basket visibly contains Panadol and vitamin
  tablets. Selling a boxed medicine through the storefront is not a decision
  to make by attaching a photo.
- **The Hello Kitty personalised box** (https://www.instagram.com/p/DbWIw6tNUVw/).
  Real product, but the licensed character is the largest thing in frame.

## One thing worth Marwan's eye

`04_chocolate_hamper.jpg` shows the confectioners' wrappers plainly — that is
simply what a chocolate hamper looks like, and the shop really does sell it.
The CADO listing text names no brand. If he would rather no third-party
packaging appeared on the storefront at all, that one photo is the one to pull.
