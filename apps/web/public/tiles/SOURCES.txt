It was black gift boxes with gold bows — the retired black-and-gold branding,
and the tile sat between Under $50 and Group Gift looking heavier than
everything around it. Marwan picked its replacement himself off the live site:
the Birthstone Pendant, a REAL product photograph already in the catalogue
(Maison Zahra Jewellers, storage product-images/95bf5cee-.../84ad8ab5-.../real.jpg).

A real product beats stock here for the same reason the hero carousels use
real products: it is a thing someone can actually buy, and it cannot go stale
into a lie. The previous file is not deleted from history — it is simply no
longer used.
